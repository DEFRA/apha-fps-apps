YearEnd Initiation

Process Rules

  • Calendar Year-End Process is a two-step process consisting of Data Setup Initiation & Approval and Year Cutover.
  • Only FPS Admin can perform Calendar Year-End process.
  • The initiator and approver for the Data Setup Initiation & Approval and Year Cutover steps must be different users.
  • On Failure or Rejection, new request needs to be submitted.
